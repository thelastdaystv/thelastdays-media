Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

"The Last Days Poster" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @TheLastDays_TV and the Instagram account @TheLastDaysTV to upload the channel's own videos.

What it accesses. With the owner's permission it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's name. It does not access anyone else's account or data.

What it stores. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. No personal data is collected, sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google API data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact. Through the YouTube channel @TheLastDays_TV.
